Wild Nippon: A Creature RPG — Support

My purchase of “The Journey Continues” is missing

On the title screen, open “The Journey Continues (Buy / Restore)” and tap “Restore Purchase”. Make sure you are signed in with the same Apple Account you used to buy it.

Moving my save to a new device

Your save is stored on your device. Restoring a new device from a backup (such as iCloud Backup) brings it over. Use “Restore Purchase” to get your purchase back.

Larger text or one-handed play

Open Menu > Settings to turn on large text, change text speed, switch to one-handed play, music, sound effects and vibration.

Contact

Please use our GitHub support page. Posts there are public, so do not include your name, email address, receipts or other personal information.
