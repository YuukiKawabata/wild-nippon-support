Wild Nippon: A Creature RPG — Privacy Policy

Effective and last updated: October 28, 2026

Summary

Wild Nippon: A Creature RPG (いきもの列島 〜ニッポン冒険記〜, “the app”) is a single-player game that runs entirely on your device. The developer (Yuki Kawabata) does not collect any personal information or usage data from the app.

Information we collect

None. The app has no account, no ads, no analytics, no crash or diagnostic reporting and no third-party SDKs. It does not connect to any server run by the developer.

Data stored on your device

Your saved game and settings (text speed, volume and so on) are stored only on your device and are never sent to the developer. Deleting the app deletes them. If they are included in a device backup such as iCloud Backup, Apple’s terms apply.

Photos, location, camera and contacts

The app does not access your photos, camera, microphone, location or contacts.

In-app purchase

“The Journey Continues” is purchased and restored through Apple’s App Store. The developer never receives your payment details. The app checks your purchase on the device using Apple’s StoreKit.

Tracking

The app does not track you across other companies’ apps or websites.

Children

Because the app collects no information, it collects none from children.

Changes

Any change to this policy will be posted on this page.
